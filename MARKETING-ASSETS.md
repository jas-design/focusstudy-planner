# FocusStudy Marketing Assets Plan

## Brand Positioning

FocusStudy is a calm, ADHD-friendly Student Planner Web App for college and university students who want to organize schoolwork without adding more visual noise.

Primary message:

Less overwhelm. More focus.

Secondary messages:

- Plan today.
- Focus on one thing.
- Keep school organized.
- No subscription.
- No account required.
- Works offline after download.

FocusStudy should be presented as an interactive student planning web app, not a printable PDF or static template.

## Customer

Primary customer:

US college or university student.

Likely needs:

- Keep track of several courses at once.
- See what matters today without digging through a complex planner.
- Break large assignments into smaller steps.
- Capture distracting thoughts quickly.
- Use a focus timer without opening a separate app.
- Avoid another subscription or account.
- Keep planner data local and export backups.

Avoid stereotypes or medical claims. The product is a productivity tool, not treatment.

## Core Promise

FocusStudy helps students plan the day, choose the Top 3 priorities, break work into smaller steps, and focus on one task at a time.

Short promise options for asset copy:

- Less overwhelm. More focus.
- Know what matters today.
- One calm place for schoolwork.
- Plan your day. Start your next step.

## Visual Principles

- Use the actual FocusStudy interface as the hero.
- Keep each listing image focused on one clear idea.
- Use warm neutral backgrounds, charcoal text, coral accents, and soft supporting colors from the product design system.
- Use modern system-style sans serif typography that feels consistent with the app.
- Keep headlines short and readable at Etsy thumbnail size.
- Avoid crowded feature grids, giant paragraphs, excessive decoration, and mockups that hide the UI.
- Use generous safe zones around headlines, product name, and main UI.
- Use consistent screenshot treatment across all images: clean app screenshots on warm backgrounds with subtle borders or shadows.
- Do not imply cloud sync, medical results, guaranteed grades, or clinical ADHD benefits.

Recommended campaign canvas:

- Etsy listing image: 2000 x 1600 px or similar 5:4 ratio.
- Background: warm neutral.
- Screenshot frame: simple, lightly rounded, subtle border/shadow.
- Text: large headline, one short support line at most.

## Marketing Claims To Avoid

Do not claim:

- Treats ADHD
- Improves ADHD symptoms
- Medically proven
- Clinically proven
- ADHD treatment
- Cures procrastination
- Guarantees better grades
- Syncs across devices
- Cloud backup
- Encrypted storage
- Used by thousands of students
- Bestseller or top-rated unless externally verified later

Prefer:

- ADHD-friendly
- Designed to reduce visual overwhelm
- Simple student planning
- Distraction-free Focus Mode
- Works offline after download
- No subscription
- No account required
- Local browser storage
- Export and import backups
